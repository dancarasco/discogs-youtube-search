Tracklist Videos for Discogs
============================

Finds every track of a Discogs release on YouTube, right in the tracklist,
and plays the whole record.

Install (Chrome, Brave, Edge or Arc)
------------------------------------
1. Unzip this file. Keep the folder somewhere it will stay (not Downloads,
   if you clear that out): Chrome loads the extension from this folder.
2. Open chrome://extensions in the address bar.
3. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the unzipped folder
   (the one with manifest.json in it).
5. Open any Discogs release, for example
   https://www.discogs.com/release/1998249
   and press "Find tracks on YouTube" above the tracklist.

Chrome may show a "Disable developer mode extensions" notice now and then.
That is normal for extensions installed this way: choose Keep / Cancel.

Using it
--------
- Each track gets its best YouTube upload beside it. Click it to play.
- "Play all" plays the record track after track in the player at the
  bottom right. The small thumbnails under the video are other uploads.
- "Open as YouTube playlist" opens every match as one playlist on YouTube.

Updating
--------
Unzip the new version over the old folder, then press the reload arrow on
the extension's card in chrome://extensions.
